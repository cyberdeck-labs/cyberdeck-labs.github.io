Privacy Policy – Cyberdeck Launcher

Also at cyberdecklabs.net/launcher/privacy/ · Last updated: 2026-11-10 · Provider: Cyberdeck Labs · Contact: lab@cyberdecklabs.net

Summary

Cyberdeck Launcher does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking service. All settings stay on your device.

What the app processes on your device

  • Installed apps: the app reads the list of launchable apps (name, icon) to display and start them. This list never leaves your device.
  • Notifications and media playback (only if you grant notification access): the title and text of your notifications and the title/artist of the music currently playing are shown so you can view, open, dismiss or control them inside the launcher. This content is neither stored nor transmitted.
  • Settings: favorites, icon style, name, wallpaper texts and the selected weather location are stored locally in the app. They are deleted when you uninstall it.
  • Battery level: only displayed.

Internet connections

  • Weather: to show the weather, the app calls the free service Open-Meteo. Only the place name you entered (location search) or that place's coordinates are sent. Your GPS location is not used. As with any internet connection, the service sees your IP address. Open-Meteo's terms: open-meteo.com/en/terms.
  • Web search: if you choose "search the web" in the search bar, the app opens your browser with a DuckDuckGo search. From then on, the terms of your browser and the search engine apply.

Permissions

  • Internet – weather and web search (see above)
  • Set wallpaper – wallpaper feature
  • Expand status bar – quick access to the system notifications
  • Request uninstall – uninstall apps from the launcher (Android asks every time)
  • Notification access – optional, see above; can be revoked at any time in the Android settings

Sharing with third parties

No data is sold or shared with third parties. The only exceptions are the connections to Open-Meteo described above and – only when you tap it – DuckDuckGo.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Since we do not store any data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose or delete. You can remove local data by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app. Questions: lab@cyberdecklabs.net.

Changes

If the app changes (for example, new online features), this page will be updated.
